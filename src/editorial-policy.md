# Editorial Policy

Viral Tweet Hub publishes educational guides for creators who want to write clearer posts on X (Twitter). This page explains how articles are selected, reviewed, updated, and corrected.

## What We Publish

We focus on practical material that helps readers improve a specific writing or publishing task:

- Hook and opening-line examples
- Thread structure and storytelling
- Engagement diagnostics
- Profile positioning
- Content calendar systems
- Analytics and weekly review habits
- Tool workflows and disclosure notes

We avoid tactics that depend on spam, impersonation, engagement pods, bought followers, misleading promises, or fake urgency.

## Topic Selection

Topics are chosen when they meet at least one of these criteria:

- The problem appears repeatedly across creator accounts
- The topic can be explained with examples or checklists
- The guide can help a reader make a better publishing decision
- The article fills a gap in the existing site library

We prefer fewer useful guides over frequent thin posts.

## Writing and Review Process

Before publication, each guide is reviewed for:

- Clear reader outcome
- Specific examples or frameworks
- Claims that avoid guaranteed results
- Formatting that works on mobile
- Relevant internal links
- Up-to-date platform language
- Independence from X Corp or Twitter

Articles should give readers something they can apply, not just broad motivation.

## Use of AI Assistance

AI tools may be used for outlining, drafting alternatives, summarizing notes, or checking structure. AI output is not treated as publish-ready.

Before an article goes live, it is reviewed and edited by a human for clarity, usefulness, originality, tone, and accuracy. Generic claims, repeated phrasing, inflated promises, and unsupported guarantees are removed during editing.

## Updates and Corrections

X changes often. Older guides may be updated when:

- Platform terminology changes
- A recommendation becomes outdated
- A better example or checklist is available
- A reader reports unclear or inaccurate material

Substantial updates may be reflected in the article copy, metadata, or related links.

## Advertising and Affiliate Links

Viral Tweet Hub may display advertising or mention third-party tools. Tool mentions are editorial resources, not guarantees. If affiliate links are added, they will be disclosed clearly near the relevant content or on the tool page.

Advertising relationships do not determine article conclusions.

## Corrections

If you find an outdated recommendation, broken link, unclear explanation, or factual issue, contact us at [hello@viraltweethub.com](mailto:hello@viraltweethub.com) with the page URL and a short description of the issue.

## Independence

Viral Tweet Hub is independent and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by X Corp, Twitter, or any social media platform.
